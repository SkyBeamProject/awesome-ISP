# Overview

A curated list of Image Signal Processor (ISP) resources: courses and books, open-source camera drivers (V4L2, Android HAL, libcamera), raw data decoders (dcraw, LibRaw, DNG SDK, MatRaw), and end-to-end and per-stage pipeline implementations and papers covering AE, BLC, LSC, DPC, AWB, demosaic, CCM, CSC, NR, HDR/tone mapping, plus datasets. Pull requests with additions or corrections are welcome.
